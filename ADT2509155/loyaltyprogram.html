How It Works

Earn As You Sip

Simple & Rewarding

Our Loyalty Programme is completely free to join. Simply ask for your loyalty card at the counter on your next visit, and start collecting stamps!

1

Get Your Card

Request a free loyalty card at the counter. No registration, no fuss.

2

Collect Stamps

Earn 1 stamp for every coffee or beverage purchased (min. RM 10).

3

Redeem Rewards

After 10 stamps, enjoy your next coffee completely FREE!

4

Keep Earning

Start a new card and continue earning rewards with every visit.

Terms & Conditions

  • Loyalty cards are issued free of charge and are non-transferable.
  • Stamps are earned on beverage purchases of RM 10 or above (excludes bottled water).
  • One stamp per transaction. Multiple drinks in one order count as one stamp.
  • Lost or damaged cards cannot be replaced. Please keep your card safe.
  • Stamps are valid for 12 months from the date of card issue.
  • Free coffee reward is valid for beverages up to RM 15 value.
  • The Daily Grind reserves the right to modify or discontinue the programme with notice.
  • Staff reserve the right to refuse stamp issuance in case of suspected fraud.
