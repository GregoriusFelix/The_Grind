Our Story

Rooted in Community,
Brewed with Passion

The Daily Grind was born in 2010 from a simple dream: to create a space where neighbours could slow down, savour great food, and feel at home. Founder Davin Lim transformed a vintage shophouse into the warm, welcoming café you see today.

We partner with local farmers, independent roasters, and artisan bakers to ensure every bite and every sip supports our community. Sustainability isn't just a buzzword here — it's written into every recipe.

From our signature flat white to our rotating seasonal menu, everything we serve is made with intention and care. We believe food should bring people together, and that's exactly what we've been doing for over 14 years.
