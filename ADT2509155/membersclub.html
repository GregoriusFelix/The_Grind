Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade my tier later?

Yes! You can upgrade anytime. Pay the difference in annual fees and the new tier benefits activate immediately.

Is the card deposit refundable?

Yes, the one-time card deposit is fully refundable when you return your member card in good condition upon cancellation.

Can I share my membership with family?

Memberships are personal and non-transferable. However, Gold and Platinum members can bring guests to exclusive events.

What happens if I lose my card?

A replacement card can be issued for a fee of RM 30. Your membership benefits remain active during replacement.

How do I cancel my membership?

Submit a written cancellation request 30 days before renewal. Annual fees are non-refundable but card deposits will be returned.
