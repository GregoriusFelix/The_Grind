Franchise Opportunities

The Daily Grind has grown from a single location to a beloved brand over 14 years. Now we're ready to share our success with passionate entrepreneurs who want to bring our community-focused cafe model to new locations across Malaysia and beyond.

Why Franchise With Us?

✓
Proven Business Model

14+ years of operational success and loyal customer base

✓
Comprehensive Training

4-week intensive training programme for you and your team

✓
Marketing Support

National campaigns, social media assets, and local PR assistance

✓
Supply Chain Access

Direct relationships with quality suppliers and preferred pricing

✓
Ongoing Support

Dedicated franchise manager and 24/7 operational assistance

✓
Brand Recognition

Leverage our established reputation and customer loyalty

Franchise Investment & Requirements

Item Details
Franchise Fee RM 150,000 (one-time)
Initial Investment RM 500,000 - RM 800,000 (includes fit-out, equipment, inventory)
Royalty Fee 6% of monthly gross revenue
Marketing Fee 2% of monthly gross revenue
Contract Term 10 years (renewable)
Location Size 1,200 - 2,000 sq ft minimum
Liquid Capital RM 300,000 minimum
Net Worth RM 1,000,000 minimum

Franchise Application Process

1
Initial Inquiry

Submit the form below expressing your interest. We'll send you an information pack within 3 business days.

2
Qualification Review

Complete our detailed application form. Our team reviews your financial qualifications and business experience.

3
Discovery Day

Visit our flagship location, meet the team, and experience operations firsthand.

4
Site Selection

We help you identify the perfect location with demographic analysis and site approval.

5
Agreement & Training

Sign the franchise agreement and complete our 4-week training programme.

6
Grand Opening

Launch your Daily Grind location with full marketing and operational support!
